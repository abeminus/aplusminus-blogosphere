If you have ever wondered what "I" means and you like mathematics and philosopy, this is the book that you should read twice. I highly recommend reading this book. Dr. Hofstadter uses plain language and beautiful analogies to describe the feedback loops that give us our "I".

I believe that this book is still in print at the time of this note, so please purchase it. You can find a preview [here at archive.org](https://archive.org/details/iamstrangeloop0000hofs/mode/2up). Please purchase a physical copy from your local bookstore or, if you really must, [at Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/Am-Strange-Loop-Douglas-Hofstadter/dp/0465030793).
