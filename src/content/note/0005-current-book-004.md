>One inconvenience I sometimes experienced in so small a house, the difficulty of getting to a sufficient distance from my guest whe we began to utter the big thoughts in big words. You want room for your thoughts to get into sailing trim and run a course or two before they make port.

>-- Henry David Thoreau, Walden, Visitors (1854).
