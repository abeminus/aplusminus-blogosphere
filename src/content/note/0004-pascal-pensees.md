[Blaise Pascal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blaise_Pascal) the philisopher that doesn't want to be called a mathmetician, had an interestimg take on infinity and ou place in it. In his [Pensées](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pens%C3%A9es), he draws a comparison between man and infinity.
>For after all what is man in nature? A nothing in regard to the infinite, a whole in regard to nothing, a mean between nothing and the whole; infinitely removed from understanding either extreme.
>
>-- Blaise Pascal, Pensées (1654).

This thought needs further exploration in a future blog post. Another thing I find insteresting in the _Pensées_ is Pascal doesn't just state the year that he wrote this in his prfession of faith at the beginning, but also the _time_ (half past ten at night). Fire.
